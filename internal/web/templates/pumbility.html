How it's worked out

{{if eq .Version.ID "phoenix2"}}

Each cleared single or double of level 10 or higher is worth a base for its level, 130 + 5 × level (plus 5 per level over 24), times its grade's multiplier plus a small bonus for its plate: 1.37 for an AA, 1.50 for an SSS+, and up to 0.02 more for a Perfect Game. A single is priced as a double one level higher. Your 50 most valuable charts count. Phoenix 2 does not publish this: PIU Scores worked it out from the official breakdowns, and grades below A, which Phoenix 2 does not publish either, are taken as B from 700,000, C from 600,000 and D from 500,000.

{{else}}

Each cleared single or double of level 10 or higher is worth a base for its level, 100 + 5 × (level − 10) × (level − 9), times a multiplier for its grade: 1 for an AA, 1.5 for an SSS+, 0.8 for an A. Plates don't count. Your 50 most valuable charts count, singles and doubles together.

{{end}}
